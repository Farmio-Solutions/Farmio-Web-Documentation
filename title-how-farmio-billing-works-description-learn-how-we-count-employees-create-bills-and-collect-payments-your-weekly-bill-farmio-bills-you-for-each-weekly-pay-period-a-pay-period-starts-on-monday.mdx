title: "How Farmio billing works"
description: "Learn how we count employees, create bills, and collect payments."

## Your weekly bill

Farmio bills you for each weekly pay period.

A pay period starts on **Monday** and ends on **Sunday**.

Your bill can include:

- A charge for each employee processed that week.
- A monthly service fee.
- Charges or credits from an earlier pay period.

Each employee is counted **once per pay period**, even if they appear on several timecards.

## When your bill is ready

You have **seven days after the pay period ends** to review and fix your timecards.

The period closes on the eighth day. Farmio creates your bill when the period closes.

You then have five days after the scheduled closing date to pay. If you have not paid, Farmio will try to charge your saved payment method.

All billing dates use **Pacific time**.

### Example

For the pay period **November 2–8, 2026**:

- **November 8:** The pay period ends.
- **November 9–15:** Review and fix your timecards.
- **November 16:** The period closes and your bill is created.
- **November 21:** Farmio tries to collect payment if you have not paid.

Closing a period early creates the bill sooner. **It does not make payment due sooner.**

If your contract has different billing dates, those dates apply.

## How employee pricing works

The number of employees processed in a pay period sets your rate.

The same rate applies to every employee in that period.

Our standard rates are:

- **1–50 employees:** \$8 per employee.
- **51–250 employees:** \$6 per employee.
- **251–750 employees:** \$4 per employee.
- **751 or more employees:** \$3 per employee.

For example, 100 employees cost **\$600** for that pay period.

There is no minimum employee charge. If no employees are processed, there is no employee charge.

All prices are in **US dollars**. If your contract has custom rates, your contract rates apply.

## The monthly service fee

The standard service fee is **\$200 per month**.

We add it to the first pay period that **starts in that month**.

For example:

- A period starting October 26 belongs to October, even if it ends in November.
- A period starting November 2 receives November’s service fee.

The fee is charged only once per month. It still applies if no employees are processed that week.

Your contract may have a different service fee.

## How to pay

Open **System Administration → Billing** to view your bill and payment date.

You can:

- Pay before the payment date.
- Wait for Farmio to charge your saved payment method.

We accept:

- US bank accounts through ACH.
- Debit cards.
- Credit cards.

**A bank account is our recommended payment method.** You may need to verify it before it can be used.

Bank payments can take several business days to finish. Your bill will show **Processing** while the payment is pending.

## If a payment fails

If payment fails, open **Billing** to review the problem and update your payment method.

A bill may show **Past due** if its payment date has passed and a balance remains.

A payment that is still processing will show **Processing**, not Past due.

## Adding employees after a period closes

A closed period can be reopened for fixes.

If you add an employee who was not on the original bill, Farmio updates the employee count and checks the rate again.

This can create an extra charge or a credit.

- If the bill has not been paid and no payment is processing, we update the bill.
- If payment is processing, we wait for the result before making an adjustment.
- If the bill was paid, we put the adjustment on a future bill.

### Why you might receive a credit

Adding an employee can move you to a lower rate.

For example:

- The original bill has 50 employees at \$8 each: \*\*\$400\*\*.
- One more employee is added.
- The new count is 51 employees at \$6 each: \*\*\$306\*\*.
- The difference is a **\$94 credit**.

If you already paid, the credit reduces a future bill. Any unused credit stays on your account.

## Who can view billing?

Organization administrators and users with the **billing permission** can view billing information and manage payment methods.
