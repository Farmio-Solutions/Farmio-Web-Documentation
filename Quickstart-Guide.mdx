## A. Quickstart Guide

### Create a new organization

1. Select the organization that you currently have open in the sidebar. 
2. From the dropdown menu, select **\+** **Create New Organization**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 06 22 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-27-at-2.06.22-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 06 22 Pm@2x")

3. Enter the new organization's name , employee size, and primary use case. Then select **Next Step**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 07 17 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-27-at-2.07.17-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 07 17 Pm@2x")

4. Select the organization's pay frequency, cycle start day, and cycle end day. Then select **Next Step**.
   - This step is optional. You may also select **Skip for now**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 08 57 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-27-at-2.08.57-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 08 57 Pm@2x")

5. [Create a crew](#id1). Then select **Proceed**.
   - This step is optional. You may also select **Skip for now**.

Your organization is now created. You will then be taken to your **organization dashboard**. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 26 33 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-27-at-2.26.33-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 27 At 2 26 33 Pm@2x")

### Add multiple employees

Upload a CSV file to add several employees at once.

To add just one employee, see [Add an employee](#ia1).

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select **\+ Add Employee**.
3. Select **Bulk Upload Employees** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 1 15 12 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-1.15.12-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 1 15 12 Pm@2x")

4. Download the [Farmio Members Template](/files/farmio-members-template.csv).
5. Open the file.
6. Delete the sample employee information. Keep the column headings.
7. Enter the information for each employee you want to add.
   - Leave the **Hourly rate** column blank to use the system's hourly rate.
8. Save the file in CSV format.
9. Upload the saved file back into Farmio.
   - The file must be 10 MB or smaller.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 22 46 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-4.22.46-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 22 46 Pm@2x")

10. Wait for the file to finish uploading. Then select **Save & Assign Members**.

### Add a crew

1. Go to the Crews page.
2. Select **\+ Add Crew**.
3. Select **Add employee** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 6 51 04 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-6.51.04-PM@2x-1.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 6 51 04 Pm@2x")

4. Give the crew a name, ID, and description.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 08 At 8 27 13 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-08-at-8.27.13-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 08 At 8 27 13 Pm@2x")

5. Assign a crew lead if this crew has one.

<Frame>
  To assign a crew lead:

  - Select **Assign Crew Lead**.

  ![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 04 59 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-7.04.59-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 04 59 Pm@2x")

  1. Choose an existing crew lead or add a new one.

  - If creating a new employee, add your employee's information.
  - Select **Save & Assign Team Lead**.

  ![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 10 23 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-7.10.23-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 10 23 Pm@2x")
</Frame>

6. You can add members to your crew in 3 ways:
   - Bulk upload new employees: see [Add multiple employees](#ia2).
   - Add a single new employee: see [Add an employee](#ia1).
   - Select your crew from existing employees:
     1. Search for an employee by name, email, or ID.
     2. Check the box next to the employees you want to add to the crew.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 22 10 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-7.22.10-PM@2x-1.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 22 10 Pm@2x")

7. Select **Add members**.
8. Confirm that all of the information is correct and select **Create Crew**.

### Add a job

1. Go to the **Jobs** page under **Workforce**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-12.37.08-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x")

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 41 00 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-12.41.00-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 41 00 Pm@2x")

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Column>
    3. Add the job details:

    - Job name
    - Job code
    - Job description
    - Pay type (hourly or piece rate)

      - If you choose hourly as the pay type, you will also enter the hourly rate.

    4. Select **Save Job**.

### Create a new time card (for a past date)

To create a time card for future work, see [Schedule a time card](#va2).

1. From the **dashboard**, elect **\+ Add Timecard.**
2. Select **Create timecard** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 22 At 8 39 20 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-22-at-8.39.20-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 22 At 8 39 20 Am@2x")

3. Enter the **ranch**, **location**, **job title**, **start time**, and **end time**.\*

_\*Remember that this is the process for creating a time card for a past date._

4. Select **Proceed** to continue**.**

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 40 28 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.40.28-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 40 28 Pm@2x")

5. Select the crew members who worked on this job.
   - Select **Available Employees** if all crew members worked on this job.
6. Select **Add Members** to save your continue. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 42 37 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.42.37-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 42 37 Pm@2x")

7. Select the edit button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-2.36.05-PM@2x.png" alt="Edit Button" noZoom style={{ width: "30px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> to fill on how many units the crew members collected.\*

_\*You can separate the units evenly across all crew members if the units are not counted by each crew member._ 

-  Select **Add New Location** and go through the process again if the crew worked in more than one location for this time card. 

8. Select **Next Step** to continue.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 43 48 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.43.48-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 43 48 Pm@2x")

9. Enter the morning, lunch, and afternoon break times for your crew. These times will apply to everyone.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 13 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.46.13-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 13 Pm@2x")

10. If someone took different breaks, select the edit button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-2.36.05-PM@2x.png" alt="Edit Button" noZoom style={{ width: "30px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to their name to change their break times.
11. Select **Update Time** to save the employee's breaks. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 45 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.46.45-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 45 Pm@2x")

12. Once all breaks are correct, select **Create Timecard** to save the timecard.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 47 19 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.47.19-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 47 19 Pm@2x")

After the time card has been saved, you will be taken to the **Timecard Details.** 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 6 48 36 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-6.48.36-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 6 48 36 Pm@2x")

<h3 id="va2">Schedule a timecard (for future work)</h3>

To create a time card for a past date, see [Create a time card](#va1).

1. From the **dashboard**, elect **\+ Add Timecard.**
2. Select **Schedule timecard** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 12 17 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.12.17-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 12 17 Pm@2x")

3. Enter the **crew**, **grower**, **ranch**, **location**, **job title**, **pay period**, and **scheduled date**.\*

   _\*Remember that this is the process for creating a time card for future work._
4. Select **Create Timecard** to schedule this timecard**.**

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 14 33 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.14.33-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 14 33 Pm@2x")

After the time card has been saved, the timecard status is set to **"scheduled"**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 17 38 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.17.38-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 17 38 Pm@2x")
