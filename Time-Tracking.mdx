## A. Creating & Viewing Time Cards

<h3 id="va1">Create a new time card (add a time card for a past date)</h3>To create a time card for future work, see [Schedule a time card](#va2).

1. From the **dashboard**, elect **\+ Add Timecard.**
2. Select **Create timecard** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 22 At 8 39 20 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-22-at-8.39.20-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 22 At 8 39 20 Am@2x")

3. Enter the **ranch**, **location**, **job title**, **start time**, and **end time**.\*

_\*Remember that this is the process for creating a time card for a past date._

4. Select **Proceed** to continue**.**

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 40 28 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.40.28-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 40 28 Pm@2x")

5. Select the crew members who worked on this job.
   - Select **Available Employees** if all crew members worked on this job.
6. Select **Add Members** to save your continue. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 42 37 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.42.37-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 42 37 Pm@2x")

7. Select the edit button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-2.36.05-PM@2x.png" alt="Edit Button" noZoom style={{ width: "30px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> to fill on how many units the crew members collected.\*

_\*You can separate the units evenly across all crew members if the units are not counted by each crew member._ 

-  Select **Add New Location** and go through the process again if the crew worked in more than one location for this time card. 

8. Select **Next Step** to continue.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 43 48 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.43.48-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 43 48 Pm@2x")

9. Enter the morning, lunch, and afternoon break times for your crew. These times will apply to everyone.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 13 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.46.13-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 13 Pm@2x")

10. If someone took different breaks, select the edit button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-2.36.05-PM@2x.png" alt="Edit Button" noZoom style={{ width: "30px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to their name to change their break times.
11. Select **Update Time** to save the employee's breaks. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 45 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.46.45-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 46 45 Pm@2x")

12. Once all breaks are correct, select **Create Timecard** to save the timecard.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 47 19 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-5.47.19-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 5 47 19 Pm@2x")

After the time card has been saved, you will be taken to the **Timecard Details.** 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 6 48 36 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-6.48.36-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 6 48 36 Pm@2x")

<h3 id="va2">Schedule a timecard (for future work)</h3>

To create a time card for a past date, see [Create a time card](#va1).

1. From the **dashboard**, elect **\+ Add Timecard.**
2. Select **Schedule timecard** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 12 17 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.12.17-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 12 17 Pm@2x")

3. Enter the **crew**, **grower**, **ranch**, **location**, **job title**, **pay period**, and **scheduled date**.\*
   _\*Remember that this is the process for creating a time card for future work._
4. Select **Create Timecard** to schedule this timecard**.**

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 14 33 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.14.33-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 14 33 Pm@2x")

After the time card has been saved, the timecard status is set to **"scheduled"**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 17 38 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.17.38-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 17 38 Pm@2x")

<h3 id="va4">Find a timecard</h3>

1. Go to the **Time Cards** page under **Time Management**.

![Image](/images/image-6.png "Image")

2. Select the pay period that you want to view.
   - The current pay period will already be filled in.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 26 05 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.26.05-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 26 05 Pm@2x")

3. Select the **Search** box above the Time Card list.
4. Enter the timecard ID, date, crew, job, grower, ranch, or location.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 32 20 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.32.20-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 32 20 Pm@2x")

</Info>

<h3 id="va5">Find an employee's timecards</h3>

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select the **Search** box above the employee list.
3. Enter the employee's first name, last name, or Employee ID.
4. Select the three dots <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.31.37-PM@2x.png" alt="Three dots menu" noZoom style={{ width: "32px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to the employee and choose **View Profile**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 25 51 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.25.51-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 25 51 Pm@2x")

5. Scroll to the bottom of the employee profile.

Under **Time Entries**, you’ll see all timecards for this employee.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 40 44 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-21-at-7.40.44-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 21 At 7 40 44 Pm@2x")

## E. Creating & Managing Time Card Templates

### Create a timecard template

1. Go to the **Templates** page under **Time Management** from the **Main Menu**. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 9 57 35 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-23-at-9.57.35-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 9 57 35 Am@2x")

2. Select **\+ Add Template**. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 00 31 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-23-at-10.00.31-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 00 31 Am@2x")

3. Enter the **template name**, **crew**, **grower**, **ranch**, **location**, **job title**, and **shift** (optional: add **breaks**.\*

_\*Remember that this is the process for creating a time card for future work._

5. Select **Create Template** to save your template. 

![Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 11 41 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-23-at-10.11.41-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 11 41 Am@2x")

---

### Create a timecard from a template

1. From the **dashboard**, elect **\+ Add Timecard.**
2. Select **Use Template** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 26 03 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-23-at-10.26.03-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 26 03 Am@2x")

3. Select all templates you want to create a timecards for.
4. Enter the dates that you want to create the timecards for. 
5. Select **Create Timecards** to save the timecards.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 28 59 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-23-at-10.28.59-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 23 At 10 28 59 Am@2x")

---
