    <h2 id="ia1">I.A.1. Add one employee</h2> Use this option to add one person.

    <Info>
      To add several people at once, see [Add multiple employees](#ia2).
    </Info>

    1. Go to the **Employees** page.
    2. Select **\+ Add Employee**.
    3. Select **Add employee** from the menu.

    ![Menu with the Add employee option](/images/add-single-employee.png)

    4. Enter the employee's information. The following fields are required:
       - **First Name**
       - **Last Name**
       - **Employee ID**

    ![Form for entering a new employee's information](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-05-at-5.08.39-PM@2x-1.png)

    5. Change the employee's **hourly rate** if needed. The system default is dependent on your location.

    ![Image](/images/image.png "Image")

    6. Select **Create Employee** to save.

    ---

    <h2 id="ia2">Add multiple employees</h2>Use this option to add two or more employees at the same time.

    <Info>
      To add one employee, see [Add one employee](#ia1).
    </Info>

    1. Go to the **Employees** page.
    2. Select **\+ Add Employee**.
    3. Select **Bulk Upload Employees** from the menu.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 1 15 12 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-1.15.12-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 1 15 12 Pm@2x")

    4. Download the [Farmio Members Template](/files/farmio-members-template.csv).
    5. Open the file.
    6. Delete the sample employee information. Keep the column headings.
    7. Enter the information for each employee you want to add.

       - Leave the **Hourly rate** column blank to use the system’s hourly rate.
    8. Save the file in CSV format.
    9. Upload the saved file back into Farmio.

       - The file must be 10 MB or smaller.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 22 46 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-4.22.46-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 22 46 Pm@2x")

    10. Wait for the file to finish uploading. Then select **Save & Assign Members**.

    <h2 id="ia3">I.A.3. Find employees by ID or organization</h2>Search for an employee by first name, last name, or Employee ID.

    1. Go to the **Employees** page.
    2. Select the **Search** box above the employee list.
    3. Enter the employee’s first name, last name, or Employee ID.

    ![Image](/images/image-1.png "Image")

    <Info>
      The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for employees, use the **Search** box on the **Employees** page.
    </Info>

    <h2 id="ia4">I.A.4. Update Employee Information</h2>Update employee information, add a profile picture, or change an employee's hourly rate.

    3. Enter the employee’s first name, last name, or Employee ID.
    4. Select the three dots <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.31.37-PM@2x.png" alt="Three dots menu" noZoom style={{ width: "28px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: "0" }} /> next to the employee and choose **"Update Profile"**.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 25 51 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.25.51-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 25 51 Pm@2x")

    5. Make your changes, then select **Update Employee** to save.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 28 03 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.28.03-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 28 03 Pm@2x")

    <Info>
      You can also edit an employee’s information from their profile. Select the three dots <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.31.37-PM@2x.png" alt="Three dots menu" noZoom style={{ width: "28px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: "0" }} /> and choose **"Update Details"**.

    <h2 id="ia5">I.A.5. Export an employee badge</h2>Download a badge with the employee's name, picture, and employee ID.

    2. Select the **Search** box above the employee list.
    3. Enter the employee’s first name, last name, or Employee ID.
    4. Select the three dots <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.31.37-PM@2x.png" alt="Three dots menu" noZoom style={{ width: "28px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: "0" }} /> next to the employee and choose **"View Profile"**.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 25 51 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-3.25.51-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 3 25 51 Pm@2x")

    5. Select **"Download Badge"** to the right of the employee's name.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 12 26 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-4.12.26-PM@2x-1.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 12 26 Pm@2x")

    <h2 id="ia6">I.A.6. Export multiple employee badges</h2>Download badges for multiple employees. Each badge includes the employee’s name, photo, and employee ID.

    1. Go to the **Employees** page.
    2. Select the checkbox next to each employee whose badge you want to download.
    3. To select all employees, select the checkbox at the top of the list.
    4. Select "\*\*Download Badges" \*\*from the bulk actions.

    ![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 3 32 32 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-3.32.32-PM@2x-1.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 3 32 32 Pm@2x")

    5. Open the downloaded file.

    <h2 id="ib1">I.B.1. Find an employee's current rate</h2> View the employee’s current pay rate.

    3. Enter the employee’s first name, last name, or Employee ID.
    4. Select the three dots next to the employee and choose **"View Profile"**.

    The employee’s current hourly rate will be found under “Rate History”.
