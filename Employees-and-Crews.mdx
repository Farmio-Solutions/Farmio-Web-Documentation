<h2 id="ia-managing-employees">A. Employees</h2> <h3 id="ia1">Add an employee</h3>

To add several employees at once, see [Add multiple employees](#ia2).

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select **\+ Add Employee**.
3. Select **Add employee** from the menu.

![Menu with the Add employee option](/images/add-single-employee.png)

4. Enter the employee's information. The following fields are required:
   - **First Name**
   - **Last Name**
   - **Employee ID**

![Form for entering a new employee's information](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-05-at-5.08.39-PM@2x-1.png)

5. Change the employee's **hourly rate** if needed. The system default is dependent on your location.

![Image](/images/image.png "Image")

6. Select **Create Employee** to save.

---

<h3 id="ia2">Add multiple employees</h3>

Upload a CSV file to add several employees at once.

To add just one employee, see [Add an employee](#ia1).

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select **\+ Add Employee**.
3. Select **Bulk Upload Employees** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 1 15 12 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-1.15.12-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 1 15 12 Pm@2x")

4. Download the [Farmio Members Template](/files/farmio-members-template.csv).
5. Open the file.
6. Delete the sample employee information. Keep the column headings.
7. Enter the information for each employee you want to add.
   - Leave the **Hourly rate** column blank to use the system's hourly rate.
8. Save the file in CSV format.
9. Upload the saved file back into Farmio.
   - The file must be 10 MB or smaller.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 22 46 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-07-at-4.22.46-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 07 At 4 22 46 Pm@2x")

10. Wait for the file to finish uploading. Then select **Save & Assign Members**.

<h3 id="ia3">Find an employee</h3>

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select the **Search** box above the employee list.
3. Enter the employee's first name, last name, or Employee ID.

![Image](/images/image-1.png "Image")

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for employees, use the **Search** box on the **Employees** page.
</Info>

<h3 id="ia6">Export multiple employee badges</h3>

Each badge includes the employee's name, photo, and employee ID.

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select the checkbox next to each employee whose badge you want to download.
3. To select all employees, select the checkbox at the top of the list.
4. Select **Download Badges** from the bulk actions.

<h2 id="ib-manage-employee-pay-rates">B. Pay rates</h2>

<h3 id="ib1">Find an employee's current rate</h3>

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select the **Search** box above the employee list.
3. Enter the employee's first name, last name, or Employee ID.
4. Select the three dots next to the employee and choose **View Profile**.

![Image](/images/image-3.png "Image")

The employee's current hourly rate will be found under **Rate History**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 4 40 58 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-4.40.58-PM@2x-3.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 4 40 58 Pm@2x")

---

<h3 id="ib2">Change an employee's rate</h3>

1. Go to the **Employees** page.
2. Select the **Search** box above the employee list.
3. Enter the employee's first name, last name, or Employee ID.
4. Select the three dots next to the employee and choose **View Profile**.

![Image](/images/image-3.png "Image")

5. Under Rate History, select the **"+"** button to add a new rate.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 5 05 36 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-5.05.36-PM@2x-2.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 5 05 36 Pm@2x")

6. Enter the new rate and its start date.
   - If the rate should end on a certain date, enter an end date. Otherwise, leave the end date blank.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 5 07 18 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-5.07.18-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 5 07 18 Pm@2x")

7. Save this new rate by selecting **Update Rate**.

<h3 id="id1">Create a crew</h3>

1. Go to the Crews page.
2. Select **\+ Add Crew**.
3. Select **Add employee** from the menu.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 6 51 04 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-6.51.04-PM@2x-1.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 6 51 04 Pm@2x")

4. Give the crew a name, ID, and description.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 08 At 8 27 13 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-08-at-8.27.13-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 08 At 8 27 13 Pm@2x")

5. Assign a crew lead if this crew has one.

<Frame>
  To assign a crew lead:

  - Select **Assign Crew Lead**.

  ![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 04 59 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-7.04.59-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 04 59 Pm@2x")

  1. Choose an existing crew lead or add a new one.

  - If creating a new employee, add your employee's information.
  - Select **Save & Assign Team Lead**. <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-7.10.23-PM@2x.png" alt="Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 10 23 Pm@2x" title="Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 10 23 Pm@2x" />
</Frame>

6. You can add members to your crew in 3 ways:
   - Bulk upload new employees: see [Add multiple employees](#ia2).
   - Add a single new employee: see [Add an employee](#ia1).
   - Select your crew from existing employees:
     1. Search for an employee by name, email, or ID.
     2. Check the box next to the employees you want to add to the crew.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 22 10 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-09-at-7.22.10-PM@2x-1.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 09 At 7 22 10 Pm@2x")

7. Select **Add members**.
8. Confirm that all of the information is correct and select **Create Crew**.
