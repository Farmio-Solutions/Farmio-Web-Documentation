## A. Growers

### Create a new grower

1. Go to the **Growers** page.
2. Select **\+ Add Grower**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 14 At 6 04 59 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-6.04.59-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 14 At 6 04 59 Pm@2x")

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Column>
    3. Add the grower's information:

    - Grower name
    - Grower code
    - Contact name
    - Contact email
    - Contact phone
    - Commission rate
    - State
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <div style={{ display: "flex", flexDirection: "column", justifyContent: "center", height: "100%" }}>
  <Card title="All fields are required" type="info">
    Complete all fields before saving the grower.
  </Card>
</div>
  </Column>
</Columns>

4. Select **Save Grower**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 14 At 6 07 45 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-6.07.45-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 14 At 6 07 45 Pm@2x")

---

### Find a grower

1. Go to the **Growers** page. 
2. Select the **Search** box above the growers list. 
3. Enter the growers's name, contact information, or grower ID.

![Image](/images/image-5.png "Image")

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for growers, use the **Search** box on the **Growers** page.
</Info>

---

  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for growers, use the **Search** box on the **Growers** page.

## B. Ranches

### Create a new ranch

1. Go to the **Growers** page.
2. Select the **Search** box above the growers list.
3. Enter the growers's name, contact information, or grower ID.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 8 48 23 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-15-at-8.48.23-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 8 48 23 Am@2x")

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for growers, use the **Search** box on the **Growers** page.
</Info>

4. Select the eye icon <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-15-at-10.46.56-AM@2x.png" alt="View Button" noZoom style={{ width: "32px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to the grower to view.
5. Under **Ranches**, select **\+ Add Ranch**.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 10 50 30 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-15-at-10.50.30-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 10 50 30 Am@2x")

6. Add the ranch name and ID.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 10 52 29 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-15-at-10.52.29-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 10 52 29 Am@2x")

7. Select **Save Ranch** to save your changes. 

---

### View all ranches by grower

1. Go to the **Growers** page.
2. Select the **Search** box above the growers list.
3. Enter the growers's name, contact information, or grower ID.

![Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 8 48 23 Am@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-09-15-at-8.48.23-AM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 09 15 At 8 48 23 Am@2x")

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for growers, use the **Search** box on the **Growers** page.
</Info>

4. Select the eye icon <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-15-at-10.46.56-AM@2x.png" alt="View Button" noZoom style={{ width: "32px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to the grower to view.
5. Under **Ranches**, you will see all active ranches under the grower.

### Find a ranch
