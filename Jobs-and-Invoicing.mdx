## A. Creating Invoices

### Create a new client invoice

1. Go to the **Invoicing** page under **Organization**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 03 At 3 07 45 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-03-at-3.07.45-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 03 At 3 07 45 Pm@2x")

2. Select **\+ Add Invoice**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 03 At 3 08 28 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-03-at-3.08.28-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 03 At 3 08 28 Pm@2x")

3. Select the **pay period** and **grower** you are creating the invoice for.
4. Select **Client Invoice** for the invoice type.

   _Optional: Choose a crew, employee, or date range to include only those workers or dates in the invoice._

![Clean Shot 2026 10 03 At 3 10 36 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-03-at-3.10.36-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 03 At 3 10 36 Pm@2x")

5. Select **Create Invoice**.

The invoice you created is now available to export under ALL Invoices.

6. To export, click the export button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-10-03-at-3.23.51-PM@2x-1.png" alt="Export Button" noZoom style={{ width: "30px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to the invoice you want to download.

## B. Managing Jobs

### Create a new job

1. Go to the **Jobs** page under **Workforce**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-12.37.08-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x")

2. Select **+Add Job**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 41 00 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-12.41.00-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 41 00 Pm@2x")

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Column>
    3. Add the job details:

    - Job name
    - Job code
    - Job description
    - Pay type (hourly or piece rate)
      - If you chose piece rate as the pay type, you will also choose the unit for the job.
      - If you choose hourly as the pay type, you will also enter the hourly rate.

    4. Select **Save Job**.
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <div style={{ display: "flex", flexDirection: "column", justifyContent: "center", height: "100%" }}>
  <Card title="Job name, code and pay type are required fields." type="info">
    Complete these fields before saving the job.
  </Card>
</div>
  </Column>
</Columns>

### Find a job

1. Go to the **Jobs** page under **Workforce**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-12.37.08-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x")

2. Enter the job name or ID.

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for growers, use the **Search** box on the **Growers** page.
</Info>

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 4 16 27 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-4.16.27-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 4 16 27 Pm@2x")

3. Select the job you are searching for.

### Update job details

1. Go to the **Jobs** page under **Workforce**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-12.37.08-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 12 37 08 Pm@2x")

2. Enter the job name or ID.

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for growers, use the **Search** box on the **Growers** page.
</Info>

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 4 16 27 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-4.16.27-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 4 16 27 Pm@2x")

3. Select the job you are searching for.
4. Update the job name, ID, description, pay type, hourly rate, and/or unit.
5. Select **Save Job** to save your changes.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 4 33 29 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-07-at-4.33.29-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 07 At 4 33 29 Pm@2x")
