## A. Creating & Managing Users

### Get all users

1. Under **System Administration**, select **Users**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.24.21-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x")

All users within your organizations will appear in the list under **All Users.** 

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 33 39 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.33.39-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 33 39 Pm@2x")

---

### Find a User

1. Under **System Administration**, select **Users**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.24.21-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x")

2. Select the **Search** box above the user list. 
3. Enter the user's name, email, or organization.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 38 36 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.38.36-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 38 36 Pm@2x")

<Info>
  The **Search** bar at the top of every page searches across Farmio. To search only for users, use the **Search** box on the **Users** page.
</Info>

---

### Create a new user

1. Under **System Administration**, select **Users**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.24.21-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x")

2. Select **\+ Invite User** at the top right of the page.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 41 40 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.41.40-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 41 40 Pm@2x")

3. Enter the new user's full name, email, role, and organization.
4. Select **Create Account**  to save this user. 

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 44 41 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.44.41-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 44 41 Pm@2x")

<Info>
  **Users** can only update their own details. **Admin** can update any user details, org details, and configurations. 
</Info>

5. Share the temporary password with the new user.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 5 57 24 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-5.57.24-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 5 57 24 Pm@2x")

6. The new user will receive a confirmation message to the email provided. The user will then enter the temporary password and create a new password. 

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 6 00 11 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-6.00.11-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 6 00 11 Pm@2x")

---

### Update user details

1. Under **System Administration**, select **Users**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.24.21-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 24 21 Pm@2x")

2. Select the **Search** box above the user list. 
3. Enter the user's name, email, or organization.
4. Select the edit button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-09-14-at-2.36.05-PM@2x.png" alt="Edit Button" noZoom style={{ width: "30px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> next to the user that you want to edit.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 38 36 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.38.36-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 38 36 Pm@2x")

4. Enter the user's full name, role, or organization. 
5. Select **Save**.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 51 15 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.51.15-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 51 15 Pm@2x")

---

### Change user language

1. Select the **Change Language** button <img src="/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.58.30-PM@2x.png" alt="Choose Language Button" noZoom style={{ width: "60px", height: "auto", display: "inline-block", verticalAlign: "middle", margin: 0 }} /> at the top right of the screen.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 52 41 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.52.41-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 52 41 Pm@2x")

2. Select your preferred language.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 55 06 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.55.06-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 55 06 Pm@2x")

3. Your preferred language should now appear as the language button.

![Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 57 20 Pm@2x](/images/CleanShot-2026-10-16-at-3.57.20-PM@2x.png "Clean Shot 2026 10 16 At 3 57 20 Pm@2x")
